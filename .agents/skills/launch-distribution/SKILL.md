# Launch Distribution & Sequential Channel Playbook

This skill covers the channel-by-channel mechanics of executing multi-platform product launches based on the **Martin (YC S23) $1.3M ARR Launch Engine Playbook**.

---

## 🎯 Sequential Channel Execution Matrix

Launch on platforms in this specific order. The later channels require established social proof and traction.

| Sequence Order | Channel | Target KPI / Goal | Primary Audience & Fit | Key Assets & Mechanics |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Reddit** | `50+ Upvotes` | Early adopters, technical users, ICP validation | Video/screenshots required; match subreddit culture; **NO friend upvotes/IP sharing** |
| **2** | **LinkedIn** | `200k+ Impressions` | B2B, founders, developers, sales leaders | First-sentence `...see more` hook, video/image, personal narrative, tag allies & company page |
| **3** | **Twitter / X** | `500k+ Impressions` | Tech, creators, startup ecosystem | Optimize for reposts; high-production demo video; pre-built creator repost list |
| **4** | **Product Hunt** | `Top 5 Product of Day` | Early adopters seeking new tools (High Conversion) | First slide video; 6-month cadence limit; live call with 5-10 teammates messaging contacts |
| **5** | **Hacker News** | `Front Page / 200+ Upvotes` | Engineers, technical founders, hackers | Show HN; deep technical architecture; no marketing speak; polite replies to flame comments |
| **6** | **PR / Press** | `Social Proof Engine` | Industry media, TechCrunch | Warm intros or exclusive pitches; attach article screenshots to LinkedIn/Twitter posts |

## 📘 Deep Channel Playbooks

### 1. Reddit (Goal: 50+ Upvotes, 20-30 Core Users)
- **General Advice:**
  - Excellent for testing ICP and messaging early.
  - Search for subreddits that explicitly allow self-promotion in official rules.
  - **STRICT WARNING:** Never ask friends, office mates, or Slack members to upvote your Reddit post. Reddit's anti-vote-manipulation algorithm tracks shared IP addresses and will instantly shadowban or ban your account.
  - Virality is determined by how well the subreddit's power users (high karma, mods, top 1% contributors) react.
- **The Post:**
  - Craft a magnetic, non-clickbait title hook.
  - Always include a video demo or clean product screenshots. Posts with media occupy 3x more vertical feed space.
  - Immerse yourself in the subreddit's language and vibe before writing.
- **Battle-Tested Examples:**
  - [Mac App Overlay Launch](https://www.reddit.com/r/macapps/comments/1measbu/built_a_tiny_overlay_to_use_chatgpt_anywhere_on/)
  - [Clock Face Productivity App](https://www.reddit.com/r/ProductivityApps/comments/1ri836j/i_built_a_day_planner_around_a_clock_face_instead/)

---

### 2. LinkedIn (Goal: 200k+ Impressions, 50-60 Paying Users)
- **General Advice:**
  - High conversion channel for B2B SaaS, developer tools, and founder audiences.
  - A single strong launch post can generate 250k+ impressions and 50+ paying users ($35/mo).
- **The Post Mechanics:**
  - **First Sentence Hook:** LinkedIn hides copy after 2 lines. Your first line's sole job is to compel scrollers to click `...see more`.
  - **Media Real Estate:** Always attach a video or high-res graphic for 3 extra inches of feed real estate.
  - **Narrative Options:**
    - *Option A:* Catchy, easy-to-read one-liners separated by clean line breaks.
    - *Option B:* 2-3 paragraph founder/personal story essay.
  - **Tagging Strategy:** Tag inspired friends, early users, advisors, and investors in the post & comments. Tag your official company page to boost algorithm indexing.
  - **Engagement:** React and respond to **100% of comments** within minutes of posting.

---

### 3. Twitter / X (Goal: 500k+ Impressions)
- **General Advice:**
  - **Repost Optimization:** Reposts are the single most important metric for Twitter algorithm reach. You can go viral with under 1,000 followers if you secure ~100 quality reposts.
  - Prepare a high-production launch video (budget ~$5k for a professional video editor or spend 1 week editing in Premiere/iMovie).
  - Reach out to a pre-compiled list of Twitter-famous creators and allies to retweet upon launch.

---

### 4. Product Hunt (Goal: Top 5 Product of the Day)
- **General Advice:**
  - Exceptionally high signup-to-paying conversion rates because PH users are actively hunting for new tools to test and adopt.
  - Limited to **once every 6 months**.
- **Swarmer Execution Strategy:**
  - Get a group of 5-10 teammates or founders on a live call on launch day.
  - Systematically message all contacts, Slack groups, Discord channels, and LinkedIn connections.
  - Use PhantomBuster automation to auto-accept connection requests and send post-connection launch messages.
  - First slide MUST be a captivating video demo.

---

### 5. Hacker News (Goal: Front Page & 200+ Upvotes)
- **General Advice:**
  - Front-page placement can drive hundreds of paying technical users.
  - Post Title: `Show HN: [Name] – [Plain, factual description of technical value]`.
  - **No Marketing Speak:** HN users despise corporate jargon. Provide deep technical architecture explanations, open-source code references, or plain founder videos.
  - **Comment Moderation:** Top comments are frequently critical or skeptical. Reply politely, address trade-offs honestly, and win over the silent majority reading the thread.

---

### 6. PR & Press (Social Proof Engine)
- **General Advice:**
  - Traditional press articles alone rarely convert users directly.
  - **The Social Proof Lever:** Take a screenshot of your TechCrunch or press article and use it as the main image in a LinkedIn/Twitter post. The social proof magnifies organic post reach by 3-5x.
  - **Journalist Outreach:** Obtain warm intros, or conduct deep research on TechCrunch/media reporters who cover your exact sub-niche. Offer exclusives and follow up respectfully.

## 📢 Internal Amplification System

### 1. Dedicated #amplify Channel
Create a dedicated Slack/Teams channel containing:
- Pre-written social copy for team members to customize.
- Direct links to launch posts for early likes and reposts.
- Clear timeline asks ("Please share on LinkedIn in the next 60 minutes").

### 2. Launch Day Schedule

```
06:00 AM PST ➔ Post on Twitter/X & Submit to Hacker News
09:00 AM PST ➔ Post on LinkedIn & targeted Reddit subreddits
10:00 AM PST ➔ Trigger #amplify Slack notification & send Email announcement
12:00 PM PST ➔ Direct message key creator/influencer list for quote-tweets
All Day       ➔ Respond to 100% of comments across all platforms
```
