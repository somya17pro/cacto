# 🎯 Meta Ads Creative Playbook ($200k ➔ $1.3M ARR Scale)

Once monthly platform launches hit ~$200k ARR and retention is locked in, Meta Ads serves as the primary engine to scale Cacto to $1.3M+ ARR over 6 months.

---

## 📐 Ad Campaign Framework & Angles

### Angle A: The Bio Link Killer (Problem-Awareness)
- **Target Audience:** Instagram Creators, Coaches, E-Commerce Sellers.
- **Hook Line (0-3s):** *"If you're still putting links in your Instagram bio, stop doing this immediately."*
- **Visual:** Side-by-side comparison. Left side shows scroller getting lost in a link-in-bio directory. Right side shows scroller commenting "SCALE" on a Reel and receiving an instant DM.
- **CTA:** *"Try Cacto for free today at cacto.cc."*

### Angle B: Real-Time Conversion Proof (Direct-Response)
- **Target Audience:** Digital Product Creators & Lead Generation Consultants.
- **Hook Line (0-3s):** *"How I made $4,200 from a single Reel without ever writing a blog post or website."*
- **Visual:** Founder screen recording showing live Reel comments triggering automated DMs with 85% open rate metrics.
- **CTA:** *"Set up your first Auto-DM trigger free in 60 seconds."*

### Angle C: 60-Second Setup Speed (Product-Demo)
- **Target Audience:** Busy Agency Owners & Social Media Managers.
- **Hook Line (0-3s):** *"Watch me automate my Instagram lead generation in under 60 seconds."*
- **Visual:** Timer on screen counting down from 60 to 0 while user types keyword trigger and connects Cacto.
- **CTA:** *"Claim your free waitlist invite at cacto.cc."*

## 🔄 Creative Testing & Iteration Protocol
1. **Volume:** Produce 20+ short-form vertical video ad variations (9:16 format) per month.
2. **Hook Variations:** Test 3 distinct hooks per video asset (Question, Curiosity Gap, Negative Warning).
3. **KPI Benchmarks:**
   - Outbound CTR > 2.5%
   - Cost Per Trial Signup < $15
   - Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) < $35
